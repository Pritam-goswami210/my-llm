A simple pancake recipe

You will need: one cup of flour, one egg, one cup of milk, a pinch of salt,
and a little butter for the pan.

First, put the flour in a bowl and stir in the salt. Make a hollow in the
middle and crack the egg into it. Pour in the milk a little at a time, stirring
until the batter is smooth. No lumps should remain. Heat a frying pan over
medium heat and add a small piece of butter. When the butter bubbles, pour in
enough batter to make a thin circle. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface,
then flip the pancake and cook the other side. Stack the finished pancakes on
a plate and serve them warm with honey, fruit, or a squeeze of lemon.
