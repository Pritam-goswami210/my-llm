A day at the market

The market opened at eight o'clock. Stalls lined the square, covered with
bright cloths and piled high with fruit, vegetables, cheese, and bread. An old
man sold honey in glass jars, and next to him a woman arranged bunches of
flowers by color. Children pointed at the stall selling warm pastries, and
their parents laughed while choosing which ones to buy. In the middle of the
square a musician played a fiddle, and a small crowd gathered to listen.
Everything moved at a relaxed pace. People stopped to talk, to taste a sample,
and to carry home bags heavy with good things. By noon the square was busy,
and by evening the stalls were packed away until the next market day.
