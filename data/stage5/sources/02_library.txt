The library visit

Maya pushed the heavy glass door and stepped into the library. The air inside
smelled of paper and floor wax. Shelves rose on every side, packed with books
of every size. She walked to the children's section and ran her fingers along
the spines until she found a book about the moon. At the desk, the librarian
stamped the due date slip and smiled. Maya carried the book to a wooden table
near the window and read the first page twice. She liked how the words formed
pictures in her head. Before she left, she chose two more books: one about
dinosaurs and one about how to grow tomatoes. The walk home felt shorter
because she was already thinking about what she would read next.
