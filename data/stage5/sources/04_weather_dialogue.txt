A conversation about the weather

-- Is it going to rain today?
-- The sky looks grey, so I think so. Did you bring an umbrella?
-- No, I left it at home. I thought the forecast said sunny.
-- Forecasts change. We should hurry before the first drops fall.
-- Look, there goes the first one. It landed on my sleeve.
-- Come on, the bus stop has a roof. We can wait there.
-- Good idea. I hope the rain does not last all afternoon.
-- Even if it does, the garden will be happy. The plants have been thirsty.
-- That is true. Everything looks greener after a good rain.
