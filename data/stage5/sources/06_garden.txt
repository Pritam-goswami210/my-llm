The garden in spring

In early March the garden looked asleep. The soil was cold and brown, and
nothing green showed above the ground. Then one morning Ali noticed tiny
green spears pushing up near the fence. Daffodils, planted months before as
dry bulbs, were waking up. Over the weeks, more shoots appeared: tulips,
peas, and lettuce that Ali had sown in neat rows. Bees came to visit the
flowers, moving from one blossom to the next. By May the garden was full of
color and the scent of damp earth. Ali watered the plants each evening and
pulled the weeds that tried to crowd them. A garden, he learned, rewards
patience more than it rewards speed.
