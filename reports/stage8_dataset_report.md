# Stage 8 Corpus C — Modern English Recommendations

## Purpose

Corpus C is planned as 20% of the training mix (per the corpus manifest). It should provide modern, natural English that complements the Stage 5 original documents (everyday prose) and the Hamlet corpus (Shakespearean English).

## Candidate Datasets (Research Only — No Downloads)

No datasets were downloaded. All candidates below are documented for future manual selection and download, subject to the project's rules: no network calls at runtime, no copyrighted material without authorization.

### Candidate 1: Project Gutenberg — Modern English fiction (pre-1929)

- **Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/
- **License:** Public domain (US) — works published before 1929, or author died more than 70 years ago
- **Estimated size:** 100 KB – 2 MB per book
- **Language:** English (modern, early 20th century)
- **Content type:** Novels, short stories, essays
- **Suitability:** High — clean OCR'd text, well-edited, natural dialogue and narrative. Choose a single novel or short story collection under 1 MB for CPU training.

  Example titles:
  - The Secret Garden (1911) — Frances Hodgson Burnett
  - The Velveteen Rabbit (1922) — Margery Williams
  - The Tale of Peter Rabbit (1902) — Beatrix Potter

### Candidate 2: Standard Ebooks — Curated public domain ebooks

- **Source:** https://standardebooks.org/
- **License:** Public domain (CC0 for the ebook files themselves; underlying works are public domain)
- **Estimated size:** 50 KB – 1 MB per book
- **Language:** English (modern, late 19th–early 20th century)
- **Content type:** Carefully formatted classic literature
- **Suitability:** High — professionally typeset, clean UTF-8 text, consistent formatting. Good for small-model training.

  Example titles:
  - Alice's Adventures in Wonderland — Lewis Carroll
  - The Jungle Book — Rudyard Kipling

### Candidate 3: US Government publications (public domain)

- **Source:** https://www.govinfo.gov/
- **License:** Public domain — US government works are not subject to copyright (17 U.S.C. § 105)
- **Estimated size:** Variable — 10 KB – several MB
- **Language:** English (modern, formal)
- **Content type:** Reports, speeches, hearings, regulations
- **Suitability:** Medium — good for formal/expository English, but less natural dialogue. Better as a supplement than a primary modern English source.

  Example titles:
  - White House press briefings
  - Congressional hearings
  - Federal agency reports

### Candidate 4: Project Gutenberg — Sherlock Holmes stories (1887–1927)

- **Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/
- **License:** Public domain (US)
- **Estimated size:** ~500 KB for the complete collection
- **Language:** English (modern, Victorian–Edwardian)
- **Content type:** Detective fiction, dialogue-heavy
- **Suitability:** High — excellent dialogue examples, clear prose, well-known public domain status.

  Example titles:
  - The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes (1892)
  - The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes (1894)

## Recommended Selection

For a small CPU-trained model (~822K parameters), a single public-domain novel or short story collection of 100–500 KB is appropriate. Recommended priority order:

1. **Project Gutenberg — Sherlock Holmes stories** (~500 KB, dialogue-rich, public domain)
2. **Standard Ebooks — Alice's Adventures in Wonderland** (clean, curated, public domain)
3. **Project Gutenberg — The Secret Garden** (1911, natural narrative, public domain)

Download manually, save as `data/stage8/modern_english.txt`, and update `data/manifests/corpus_manifest.json` to point at it. Do not download programmatically at runtime.
