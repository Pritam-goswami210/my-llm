# Stage 9 Vocabulary Utilization Investigation

## Objective

Investigate the vocabulary utilization metric reported in Stage 8 and distinguish between two different measurements:

1. **Input vocabulary coverage**: What fraction of the tokenizer's vocabulary actually appears in the training corpus text. This measures whether the model has the opportunity to learn each token.

2. **Prediction utilization**: What fraction of the tokenizer's vocabulary the model predicts (argmax of logits) during forward passes. This measures output behavior of a specific trained/untrained model.

These are DIFFERENT metrics. A low prediction utilization does NOT mean the tokenizer is inadequate — it may simply mean the model is untrained and hasn't learned to predict most tokens yet.

## 3. Analysis

### Key Finding: These measure different things

The Stage 8 `vocab_utilization()` function measures **prediction utilization** — which tokens the model actually predicts (argmax of logits) during forward passes. For an untrained or barely trained model, the model's predictions are essentially random and concentrated on a few frequent tokens. This does NOT indicate a problem with the tokenizer.

### Input coverage vs prediction utilization

Input coverage tells you whether the tokenizer can represent all characters in your training data. If a character in your corpus maps to `UNK`, the model cannot learn it — that's a real problem.

Prediction utilization tells you which tokens the model currently guesses most often. For an untrained model, this will be low regardless of tokenizer quality. As training progresses, the model learns to predict more diverse tokens and utilization increases naturally.

### Recommendation

1. **Track input vocabulary coverage** as a tokenizer quality metric. Ensure 100% of training corpus characters are covered (0 unknown tokens).

2. **Track prediction utilization as a training diagnostic**, not a tokenizer quality metric. Low utilization early in training is expected; it should increase as the model learns. Stagnant low utilization may indicate a learning problem, not a tokenizer problem.

3. **Use BPE tokenizer** (from Stage 9 Phase A decision) — it has 0 unknown characters and covers all byte values, so input coverage is guaranteed at 100% for any valid UTF-8 text.
