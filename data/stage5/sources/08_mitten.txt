The lost mitten

It was a cold December afternoon when Leo realized his mitten was missing. He
had walked to school wearing both of them, but now only the left one remained
on his hand. Leo retraced his steps: the bus stop, the slide in the park, the
bench where he had tied his shoelaces. At each place he asked the other
children, but nobody had seen it. Just as he was about to give up, the school
caretaker appeared holding a red mitten. Someone had found it near the slide
and brought it inside. Leo said thank you and pulled the mitten back onto his
hand. The walk home felt warmer than the walk to school had been.
