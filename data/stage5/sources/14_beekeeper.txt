Interview with a beekeeper

-- How did you start keeping bees?
-- My grandfather had hives, and I used to watch him. When I was older, I got
  my own hive and learned as I went.
-- What do bees do all day?
-- They fly from flower to flower, collecting nectar and pollen. Back at the
  hive, they turn nectar into honey and store it in wax combs.
-- Do bees ever sting?
-- They can, but they usually only sting when they feel threatened. Most of
  the time they are busy with their work.
-- How do you get the honey?
-- We carefully remove the honeycomb frames, spin them in a machine to draw
  out the honey, and then strain it into jars. Nothing is added or taken away.
-- Why are bees important?
-- As they move between flowers, they carry pollen. That helps many plants
  produce fruit and seeds, including much of the food we eat.
